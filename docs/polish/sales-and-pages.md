# Polish pass: sales and info pages

Routes: `/firms`, `/clinics`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/book`, `/book/check` (new), `/ai`, `/privacy`, `/notes`,
`/notes/google-ads-leads-button-clicks`, `/notes/new-patients-from-website-and-ads`.
Judged at 390 first, then 360, 768, 1024 and 1440. No visible wording was changed.

Evidence lives in `output/polish-sales/` (`before/` and `after/`, not committed). Frame sheets are slowed
recordings (time labels are animation milliseconds). Every form send was answered by the capture script
itself (`page.route('**/api/**')`); no request reached `/api/contact`.

## Checked and left as they are

- Scroll reveals (`data-reveal`): every block finishes within about 200px of entering on a phone and nothing rests half drawn (`before/scroll/firms-band-phone.jpg`; the system agent has since capped the range at `min(55%, 30vh)`).
- The notes contents list: sticky on wide screens, pinned smoothly, leaves with the article, never overlaps the closing section (`after/scroll/note-contents-end-desktop.jpg`). It does not highlight the current section; adding that would be a new feature, so it is a suggestion for Kareem rather than a fix.
- Controls audit: every route clean at phone and desktop (target sizes, names, keyboard walk, overflow at 360, links, images): `after/controls-*.txt`.
- Motion audit (dev server): 0 dropped frames on every route on a phone, at most 0.6% on a desktop scroll. What remains off the compositor in my area is small colour, underline and ring changes, and the FAQ panel's height with its fade, which the brief sanctions for disclosures: `after/motion-*.txt`.
- Layout at 360, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440: no horizontal scroll, no button text wrapping, no clipping.
- `npx tsc --noEmit` clean; `npx eslint` clean on all my folders; no em or en dashes in my files.
